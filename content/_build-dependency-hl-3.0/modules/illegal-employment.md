- 3.1.1. *Illegal Immigration Employment*: Knowingly employ, recruit, or contract individuals who are not legally authorized to work in the jurisdiction where the work is performed, in violation of applicable immigration and employment laws, including but not limited to the Immigration Reform and Control Act of 1986 (IRCA) in the United States, the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 in the United Kingdom, or equivalent applicable law;
