- 3.1.1. *Forcible Military Conscription*: Be an individual or entity, or a representative, agent, affiliate, successor, attorney, or assign of an individual or entity, that engages in, facilitates, or provides material support for:

  - 3.1.1.1. The forcible abduction, detention, or coercion of individuals for the purpose of compulsory military service without due process of law;

  - 3.1.1.2. The operation of extralegal conscription enforcement activities, including but not limited to the apprehension of individuals in public spaces, workplaces, or private residences for immediate military induction without valid judicial authorization;

  - 3.1.1.3. The denial of legally established exemptions, deferrals, or conscientious objector status to individuals entitled to such protections under applicable law; or

  - 3.1.1.4. The use of threats, violence, or intimidation to compel military service in contravention of Article 8 of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (prohibition of forced or compulsory labor) and Article 4 of the European Convention on Human Rights;
