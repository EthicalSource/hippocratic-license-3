- 3.1.1. *Designated Terrorist Organizations*: Be an individual or entity, or a representative, agent, affiliate, successor, attorney, or assign of an individual or entity:

  - 3.1.1.1. Designated as a Foreign Terrorist Organization (FTO) by the U.S. Department of State pursuant to Section 219 of the Immigration and Nationality Act;

  - 3.1.1.2. Designated as a Specially Designated Global Terrorist (SDGT) by the U.S. Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC);

  - 3.1.1.3. Listed on the United Nations Security Council Consolidated List maintained pursuant to Security Council resolutions 1267 (1999), 1989 (2011), and 2253 (2015);

  - 3.1.1.4. Designated as a terrorist organization or entity by the European Union pursuant to Council Common Position 2001/931/CFSP; or

  - 3.1.1.5. Proscribed as a terrorist organization under the United Kingdom's Terrorism Act 2000;
