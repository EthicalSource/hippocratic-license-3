- 3.1.1. *Animal Cruelty*: Be an individual or entity, or a representative, agent, affiliate, successor, attorney, or assign of an individual or entity, that:

  - 3.1.1.1. Has been convicted of, or pleaded guilty or no contest to, any felony or indictable offense involving the intentional torture, mutilation, maiming, or killing of an animal, animal fighting, or bestiality, under applicable law, including but not limited to the Animal Welfare Act (7 U.S.C. § 2131 et seq.) in the United States, the Animal Welfare Act 2006 in the United Kingdom, or equivalent applicable law; or

  - 3.1.1.2. Has been found liable by a court or competent administrative tribunal for violations of animal welfare laws involving willful neglect causing serious injury or death, or the operation of illegal animal fighting enterprises;
